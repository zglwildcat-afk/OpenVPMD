
# OpenVPMD  
# 可变磁极对数旋转磁场无级变速电磁传动装置（开源项目）

## 1. Project Overview  
OpenVPMD is an open‑source implementation of a continuously variable electromagnetic transmission system based on a variable pole number rotating magnetic field.

## 项目简介  
OpenVPMD 是一个基于“可变磁极对数旋转磁场”原理的开源无级变速电磁传动装置项目。

---

## 2. Origin of the Idea  
This project is inspired by the publicly disclosed patent:  
Zhang Xueliang, *“Variable Pole Number Rotating Magnetic Field Continuously Variable Electromagnetic Transmission Device”*, CN108716530A.

## 原始构想来源  
本项目灵感来自已公开的专利：  
张学良，《可变磁极对数旋转磁场无级变速电磁传动装置》，专利号：CN108716530A。

---

## 3. Project Goals  
- Enable any engineer to build a prototype based on publicly available information  
- Demonstrate continuously variable speed using variable pole magnetic fields  
- Demonstrate electromagnetic clutching without mechanical contact  
- Promote open‑source development of electromagnetic transmission technology  

## 本项目目标  
- 让任何工程师都可以基于公开资料制造出样机  
- 展示可变磁极对数旋转磁场实现的无级变速  
- 展示无需机械接触的电磁离合功能  
- 推动电磁传动技术的开源发展  

## 4. ## 8. Vision  
This project carries the lifelong dream of its inventor.  
In his youth, he worked as a car mechanic. For more than twenty years, he has been imagining an electromagnetic continuously variable transmission that could replace the traditional automotive gearbox.

Although the idea is simple and elegant, it did not pass the patent examination.  
Without the financial ability to continue the patent process, he chose to open the idea to the world instead of letting it disappear.

Today, at the age of 53, he sincerely hopes that engineers and innovators around the world can help turn this concept into a real product.  
If one day this electromagnetic CVT can be built, and he can personally drive a vehicle equipped with it even once, that would fulfill his lifelong dream.

This open‑source project is his invitation to the world:  
to use collective intelligence to change the future of automotive technology.
